📋 Statutory Rules & Circulars

NEW
RULE 4(4) • E-COMMERCE

Every e-commerce entity must appoint a Nodal Officer and acknowledge consumer grievances within 48 hours.

RBI CIRCULAR • UPI / IMPS

Harmonisation of Turn Around Time (TAT): Banks must auto-reverse failed UPI debits within T+1 day or pay ₹100/day penalty.

SECTION 2(11) • CP ACT 2019

Withholding legitimate refunds or delivering damaged/wrong items constitutes formal Deficiency in Service.
